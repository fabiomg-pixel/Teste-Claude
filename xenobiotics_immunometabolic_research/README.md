# Xenobiotics-Immunometabolic Research Framework

## Research Question
Identify xenobiotics that:
1. Have documented effects on immunometabolic pathways
2. Accumulate in blood at concentrations matching bioactive levels in contaminated populations
3. Are present in areas endemic for arboviral diseases (dengue, Zika, chikungunya, yellow fever)

## Rationale
Environmental exposures may modulate immune-metabolic crosstalk, potentially affecting:
- Innate immune responses to viral infection
- Inflammatory cytokine production
- Cellular metabolism (glycolysis, oxidative phosphorylation)
- Trained immunity and immune tolerance

## Framework Overview

### Phase 1: Literature Mining for Immunometabolic Effects
**Target Pathways:**
- mTOR/AMPK signaling
- HIF-1α activation
- NF-κB/inflammasome
- PPAR-γ/lipid metabolism
- Glycolytic reprogramming (Warburg effect in immune cells)
- Mitochondrial dysfunction
- Aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AhR) - key xenobiotic sensor

### Phase 2: Biomonitoring Data Integration
**Concentration Matching Criteria:**
- Environmental exposure levels (μg/L or ng/mL in blood/serum)
- In vitro effective concentrations from mechanistic studies
- Margin of exposure (MOE) analysis

### Phase 3: Geographic Prioritization
**Arboviral Endemic Regions with Industrial/Agricultural Activity:**
- Amazon basin (Brazil) - mercury, pesticides
- Mekong Delta (Vietnam) - Agent Orange legacy, pesticides
- West Africa - lead, e-waste, pesticides
- Central America - pesticides, heavy metals
- South/Southeast Asia - industrial pollution, pesticides

## Priority Compound Classes

### Tier 1: Strong Immunometabolic Evidence + High Exposure
1. **PFAS (per/polyfluoroalkyl substances)**
   - Immunotoxic, affects antibody responses
   - Ubiquitous contamination, long half-life
   - Military bases, industrial sites globally

2. **Dioxins/PCBs**
   - AhR activation → immune modulation
   - Persistent, bioaccumulative
   - Legacy contamination in Vietnam, USA, Europe

3. **Arsenic**
   - Disrupts macrophage function
   - Groundwater contamination: Bangladesh, India, South America
   - Endemic dengue overlap

4. **Organochlorine pesticides (DDT, lindane)**
   - Still used for malaria control
   - Immunomodulatory effects documented
   - Perfect geographic overlap with arboviral regions

### Tier 2: Emerging Concern
5. **Glyphosate**
   - Most used herbicide globally
   - Gut microbiome disruption → immune effects
   - Agricultural regions overlap with dengue

6. **Phthalates/BPA**
   - Endocrine disruption with immune effects
   - Ubiquitous exposure
   - Urban areas in endemic regions

7. **Particulate matter (PM2.5)**
   - Inflammatory, metabolic disruption
   - Urban areas in South Asia, Africa

### PubMed Query Templates
See `search_queries.md` for detailed Boolean search strings.

### Inclusion Criteria
1. Primary research or systematic reviews
2. Quantitative exposure-response data
3. Human biomonitoring OR in vitro at relevant concentrations
4. Specific immunometabolic endpoints measured

### Exclusion Criteria
1. High-dose acute toxicity only
2. No mechanistic data
3. Non-peer reviewed sources
